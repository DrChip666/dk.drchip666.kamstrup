See what your home uses right now, not a minute ago. smart-me Local reads your Kamstrup electricity meter through the smart-me module directly on your own network, so power updates every few seconds without a detour through the cloud, and Homey Energy shows your whole home's import and export.

Voltage and current on each phase are there too. Use them in Flows to keep an eye on your main fuse, for example to pause an EV charger or a heat pump before a phase gets overloaded. Modbus TCP must be enabled for the module in the smart-me portal, which requires a smart-me Professional licence.
